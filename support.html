Frequently Asked Questions

Is Fluid 101 medical advice?

No. Fluid 101 provides educational hydration guidance. Always consult a healthcare professional for medical decisions.

Does the app collect my health data on a server?

No. Tracker data and preferences are stored locally on your device only. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I change language or theme?

In the mobile app, use the language and palette icons in the top app bar. Settings are saved on your device.

How do I delete my data?

Use Delete All Local Data in the app Settings screen, or the button below for website data stored in your browser.

Delete Local Data

If you want to remove all data Fluid 101 has saved on this device or browser, you can do so below. This will reset preferences and tracker information stored locally.

Mobile app: Open Settings → Delete All Local Data.

Website: Click the button below to clear browser storage for this site.
