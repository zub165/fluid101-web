In-app deletion (mobile)

  1. Open the Fluid 101 app.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete All Local Data.
  4. Confirm deletion. Tracker data, language, and theme preferences are removed from this device.

Fluid 101 does not require an account and does not store your hydration data on our servers.

Web / email deletion

To clear browser storage for this site, use the button below. You can also email zub165@yahoo.com with subject Delete My Data.

What is removed vs what stays

  • Removed: local hydration tracker values, language/theme preferences, and website localStorage/sessionStorage for this site.
  • Stays on device: nothing server-side; uninstalling the app also removes remaining local app data.
